The Partnership Case

Why This
Makes Sense.

Zero Opportunity Cost

DYT Breakfast Club sessions run at 7:30–9:30 AM — hours that generate no bookings for the facility. Roehampton loses no existing revenue by partnering with us. Every session we hold there during those hours is revenue and exposure they would not otherwise receive.

Direct Revenue Already Generated

On pay-to-play, DYT brings 10+ people per session at £5 each. That is direct, immediate revenue during dead hours. As the DYT community grows, those numbers grow — more people, more sessions, more revenue flowing directly to Roehampton.

Proven Indirect Impact

The price of pay-to-play at Roehampton has risen from £3 to £5 over the period SBB has been creating content there. Demand has increased. That increased demand did not come from nowhere — content driving millions of people to ask where this court is has contributed directly to that growth.

The Long-Term Vision

DYT is growing. The influential figures of British basketball attend these sessions. As DYT scales, Roehampton becomes the permanent home of UK basketball's most prominent community. The marketing value of that association is not measurable in a £65/hour court hire fee.
